Sales happens in WhatsApp. So does the busywork.

Sales agents answer the same questions all day, lose leads when they can't reply fast enough, and forget follow-ups when things get busy. EasyJarvin takes that load off them.

What EasyJarvin does

It works alongside your sales agents in the conversations they already have.

Drafts replies

Suggests on-brand answers in seconds, in the tone your team uses with clients.

Qualifies leads

Asks the right questions and flags who is ready to buy, so agents focus on the hottest leads.

Answers FAQs

Handles prices, services and availability questions using your business's own information.

Follows up

Reminds and re-engages clients who went quiet, so no lead is forgotten.

Summarizes chats

Turns long threads into short summaries so anyone on the team can pick up where it left off.

Books next steps

Moves conversations toward a meeting, a consultation or a sale.

How it works

Set up once, then let EasyJarvin work in the background of every conversation.

Connect WhatsApp

Link the sales agent's WhatsApp so EasyJarvin can see incoming client messages.

Teach it your business

Add your services, prices, FAQs and sales style. EasyJarvin uses them in every reply.

Sell faster

EasyJarvin drafts, qualifies and follows up while your agent stays in control and approves what goes out.
